Student guide

Plan your schedule with Class Planner

Class Planner uses course times, your preferences, and your personal commitments to build schedule options. You can compare them before you enroll.

When your schedule is ready, Class Planner can open each course in TSS so you can finish booking.

Check every option. Course times may be missing or listed as TBA, and seats can change.

1 Build your plan

Find courses you want to take

Choose a term. Then search by course code or title. You can search with part of a course name or number, and you do not need to type leading zeroes.

  • Open View section details to compare instructors, meeting times, rooms, related labs, and current open seats before you add a course.
  • Add 1 to 12 courses.
  • Seat and waitlist counts can change. They do not save you a spot.

2 Set preferences

Tell the planner what works for you

Choose Open or waitlist or Open only. Then rank these goals: Fewer days, Later starts, and Shorter gaps.

  • Set class-time limits with Start no earlier and Finish by.
  • Add the days and times when you would prefer to take classes.
  • Add fixed times in your personal schedule as weekly or specific-date commitments.

Add your personal schedule and commitments

Add times when you are busy, such as work, practice, appointments, or other plans. Class Planner will not place classes during those times.

A preferred time is when you would rather take a class. A commitment is a time when you are not available.

Choose Add preferences before you build. You can change these settings later from Preferences.

3 Compare and review

Compare your schedule options

Use Previous and Next to move through your proposed schedules. Best match based on your priorities is the option that best fits your settings. Other options may also work.

Read the full calendar

The calendar shows every class meeting. Finals Week appears below it, so check both before you choose.

Choose a schedule view

Use Calendar for your week, Details for course information, and Map for the route between classes.

Check the review status

No conflicts means no review items were reported.

Review items apply to the schedule that is on screen.

If a schedule is marked Best-effort, review it before booking. You can keep editing or continue after you have checked it.

Map view

Know if you can make it across campus

Switch to Map to see each day’s classes in order. Class Planner shows the route, estimated walking time, and distance between classes.

  • Choose a day to see its route and class stops.
  • Check how much time you have between classes.
  • See a warning when the gap is tight or classes overlap.

Walking times are estimates. Leave extra time when you need it.

4 Adjust sections

Keep the sections you like and try other options

Select a course block to see its details. Choose Lock Course to keep its current sections while you view other schedules. Unlock it when you want Class Planner to try different sections.

Try a different set of sections

Swap Section lets you compare other lecture, discussion, and lab groups before you change your schedule.

Current
The sections now on your schedule.
Fits current schedule
The sections do not create a time conflict.
Preview
Shows these sections on your calendar without saving them.
Use this section
Adds these sections to your schedule.

Seat counts can change. Previewing or choosing sections does not save you a seat.

Check schedule details

Open Details to see meeting and exam times, rooms, instructor, seats, waitlist, and section ID. It also shows when the information was last updated.

If information is missing or old, check it in TSS before you decide.

5 Finish and book

Finish your plan and continue to TSS

Choose Finish & book when your schedule is ready. Class Planner saves the schedule and gives you a link for each course in TSS.

  1. Sign in to TSS.
  2. Choose Open in TSS for a course.
  3. Check the course and the sections you planned.
  4. Choose Go to Booking, then Book.
  5. Return to Class Planner and open the next course.

Opened in TSS only means the link was opened. It does not mean the course was booked.

i

Remember: Class Planner does not enroll you

Opening a course in TSS is not enrollment. Check the term, course, sections, and seat information before you book. Class Planner does not save you a seat.

Save, return, and add it to your calendar

Choose Copy your return link so you can come back later. Anyone with the link can view the saved schedule. On the saved page, you can choose Edit, Details, or Book in TSS.

Add to Calendar can download a calendar file or open Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. It copies the schedule once and does not update when course information changes.

If TSS links are not available, the final button may say Save & share. You can still save the schedule and use its section details to finish in TSS.

Frequently asked questions

Know before you plan

Does Class Planner enroll me in classes?

No. Class Planner can open each course in TSS, but you still need to sign in, check your sections, choose Go to Booking, and choose Book.

What does Open in TSS do?

It opens the matching course page in TSS. Check the course and planned sections before you book. Opened in TSS only means the link was opened.

Why do I see Save & share instead of Finish & book?

Some courses may not have a TSS booking link. You can still save and share the schedule, then use the section details to finish in TSS.

Are seats or waitlist positions guaranteed?

No. Seat and waitlist counts can change. Class Planner does not save a seat or waitlist spot.

What should I do with a Review warning?

Open Review and check the items for the schedule on screen. Change the schedule or check the course in TSS if needed.

What if a course has TBA or missing meeting information?

Class Planner cannot check a meeting time it does not have. Look up the course in TSS and leave room for the missing meeting.

Can I return to or share a saved schedule?

Yes. Choose Copy your return link after you finish. Anyone with the link can view the saved schedule. Choose Edit to make changes.

Can I add the schedule to my calendar?

Yes. Choose Add to Calendar, then pick a calendar app or download the calendar file. This copies the schedule once. It does not update when course information changes.

How many courses can I add?

You can add 1 to 12 courses.
